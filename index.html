A SLOW, VERIFIED CASE · 2021 – 2025

世界，正在慢慢變好
The World Is Slowly Getting Better

新聞頭條每天提醒我們世界的傷口，卻很少報導傷口正在癒合。以下十二則紀錄，取材自過去五年，並經聯合國、世界衛生組織、國際能源總署等機構的資料驗證——它們共同描繪出一條緩慢卻真實的上升曲線。

Headlines remind us daily of the world's wounds, but rarely report on their healing. The twelve records below are drawn from the past five years and verified against data from the WHO, the UN, the IEA, and other institutions — together they trace a slow but real upward curve.

2021

全球首支瘧疾疫苗問世The World's First Malaria Vaccine

世界衛生組織正式建議在瘧疾中高傳播地區為兒童施打 RTS,S/AS01 疫苗——這是人類史上第一支獲推薦大規模使用的瘧疾疫苗。迦納、肯亞與馬拉威的試點計畫顯示，接種兒童的死亡率下降 13%，重症住院率下降 22%。

The WHO recommended RTS,S/AS01 for children in regions of moderate-to-high malaria transmission — the first malaria vaccine ever endorsed for widespread use. Pilot data from Ghana, Kenya, and Malawi showed a 13% drop in child deaths and a 22% drop in severe-malaria hospitalizations.

WHO, 2021 →
2023

第二支瘧疾疫苗獲推薦，供應瓶頸解除A Second Malaria Vaccine Clears the Supply Bottleneck

世衛組織推薦牛津大學研發的 R21/Matrix-M 疫苗，成本更低、產能更大，解決了第一支疫苗長期供不應求的問題。截至 2024 年，已有至少 28 個非洲國家計畫將瘧疾疫苗納入常規免疫接種計畫。

The WHO recommended Oxford's R21/Matrix-M vaccine — cheaper and easier to produce at scale — resolving years of supply shortage for the first vaccine. By 2024, at least 28 African countries planned to add a malaria vaccine to routine immunization.

WHO, 2023 →
2023–24

新增愛滋病毒感染數，創 1980 年代以來新低Fewer New HIV Infections Than Any Year Since the 1980s

2023 年全球新增愛滋病毒感染人數約 130 萬，較 2010 年下降 39%；愛滋相關死亡人數則較 2010 年腰斬，從 130 萬降至約 63 萬。全球逾四分之三的愛滋病毒感染者已接受抗病毒治療。

An estimated 1.3 million people acquired HIV in 2023 — 39% fewer than in 2010 — while AIDS-related deaths fell by nearly half over the same period, from 1.3 million to about 630,000. More than three in four people living with HIV are now on treatment.

UNAIDS →
2024

五歲以下兒童死亡率降至歷史新低Under-Five Child Mortality Falls to a Record Low

聯合國兒童死亡率估計機構間小組（UN IGME）指出，全球五歲以下兒童死亡率自 1990 年以來下降了 60%，從每千名活產 93.5 人降至 37.4 人——意味著一個孩子活到五歲的機會，比三十多年前高出許多。

According to the UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation, the global under-five mortality rate has fallen 60% since 1990 — from 93.5 to 37.4 deaths per 1,000 live births — meaning a child's odds of reaching age five are far better than a generation ago.

UNICEF / UN IGME, 2024 →
2024

埃及、維德角獲認證根除瘧疾Egypt and Cabo Verde Certified Malaria-Free

世界衛生組織正式認證埃及與維德角為「無瘧疾國家」，需連續三年境內零本土傳播病例方能取得此認證。2025 年喬治亞、蘇利南與東帝汶相繼跟進，全球累計已有 47 個國家與地區獲得此殊榮。

The WHO certified Egypt and Cabo Verde as malaria-free — a status requiring at least three consecutive years without indigenous transmission. Georgia, Suriname, and Timor-Leste followed in 2025, bringing the global total to 47 countries and territories.

WHO →
2024

亞馬遜雨林砍伐率降至九年新低Amazon Deforestation Falls to a Nine-Year Low

巴西國家太空研究院（INPE）數據顯示，2023 至 2024 年度亞馬遜雨林砍伐面積年減 30.6%，創下九年來最低紀錄；到 2025 年，砍伐率已連續第三年下降。

Data from Brazil's National Space Research Institute (INPE) showed Amazon deforestation fell 30.6% year-on-year in 2023–24, the lowest level in nine years — and by 2025, the rate had declined for a third consecutive year.

Mongabay / INPE, 2024 →
2024

臭氧層持續癒合，步入正軌The Ozone Layer Keeps Healing, on Schedule

聯合國報告指出，2024 年南極臭氧層破洞是 1992 年以來第七小的紀錄，形成得慢、癒合得也快。在《蒙特婁議定書》推動下，超過 99% 的臭氧層破壞物質已被淘汰，北極臭氧層預計 2045 年、南極則於 2066 年前恢復至 1980 年前的水準。

A UN report found the 2024 Antarctic ozone hole was the seventh-smallest since 1992, forming slowly and closing quickly. Under the Montreal Protocol, over 99% of ozone-depleting substances have been phased out; the Arctic ozone layer is on track to fully recover by 2045, the Antarctic by 2066.

UN News, 2024 →
2024

全球平均壽命回升至疫情前水準Global Life Expectancy Recovers to Pre-Pandemic Levels

COVID-19 疫情曾在 2021 年將全球平均壽命拉低到 70.9 歲，但到 2023–2024 年已回升至 73.2–73.3 歲，幾乎所有國家與地區的壽命都已恢復或超越疫情前水準。

Global life expectancy at birth, dragged down to 70.9 years by COVID-19 in 2021, climbed back to 73.2–73.3 years by 2023–2024 — with nearly every country and area now matching or exceeding its pre-pandemic level.

2024

全球太陽能裝置量連續 22 年刷新紀錄Global Solar Additions Set a Record for the 22nd Year Running

國際能源總署（IEA）指出，2024 年全球新增太陽能發電裝置容量達 553 GW，年增 30%，是成長最快的發電技術；全球再生能源新增裝置量也連續第 22 年刷新歷史紀錄。

The IEA reported 553 GW of new solar capacity added globally in 2024 — up 30% year-on-year and the fastest-growing power technology — marking the 22nd consecutive year that renewable capacity additions set a new record.

IEA, 2024 →
2024

婚姻平權持續擴張Marriage Equality Keeps Expanding

愛沙尼亞（2024 年生效）成為第一個承認同性婚姻的後蘇聯國家；希臘成為第一個立法承認同性婚姻的東正教國家；列支敦斯登與泰國也在同年通過相關法案，泰國成為亞洲第二個實現婚姻平權的國家。

Estonia became the first post-Soviet country to recognize same-sex marriage (effective 2024); Greece became the first Orthodox Christian country to legalize it; Liechtenstein and Thailand passed their own laws the same year, making Thailand the second country in Asia with marriage equality.

2025

幾內亞龍線蟲病：僅剩 10 例，史上最低Guinea Worm Disease: Just 10 Cases Left, an All-Time Low

2025 年全球僅通報 10 例人類幾內亞龍線蟲病病例，較 2024 年再減 33%。1986 年卡特中心接手根除計畫時，全球估計每年有 350 萬病例；如今這項疾病有望成為繼天花之後，人類第二個徹底根除的疾病。

Only 10 human Guinea worm cases were reported worldwide in 2025 — down 33% from 2024, and the lowest number ever recorded. When the Carter Center took over the eradication campaign in 1986, an estimated 3.5 million cases occurred annually; the disease is now on the verge of becoming only the second in history, after smallpox, to be eradicated.

The Carter Center, 2025 →
2025

再生能源首度超越煤炭，成為全球最大電力來源Renewables Overtake Coal as the World's Largest Power Source

國際能源總署數據顯示，2025 年上半年，全球再生能源發電量首次超越煤炭，成為單一最大電力來源——比先前預測的時程還要提前，標誌著全球能源結構的一個歷史轉折點。

IEA data showed that in the first half of 2025, renewables overtook coal to become the world's single largest source of electricity generation for the first time — earlier than previously forecast, marking a historic turning point in the global energy mix.

IEA, 2025 →

進步不是必然，也不是均勻分布的——它是無數決策、資金與長年堅持工作換來的結果，許多地方仍在等待這些曲線抵達。閱讀這些數字，不是要我們停止批判，而是提醒我們：改變確實正在發生，而且是可以被測量、被驗證、被延續的。

Progress is neither inevitable nor evenly distributed — it is the outcome of countless decisions, funding, and years of sustained work, and many places are still waiting for these curves to reach them. These numbers are not a reason to stop being critical; they are a reminder that change is real, measurable, and worth continuing.
